## 📌 Summary
Multiplayer AI documents. Building real-time collaborative workspaces where multiple human team members and AI agents co-edit the same document with CRDTs (Yjs) and presence indicators.

## 🎯 Key Takeaways
- CRDTs (Yjs) resolve concurrent edits between multiple human users and streaming AI agents without conflicts
- Liveblocks synchronizes shared awareness state: show user presence cursors and active AI generation highlights
- Enables human reviewers to highlight specific paragraphs and prompt the AI to rewrite in real time
